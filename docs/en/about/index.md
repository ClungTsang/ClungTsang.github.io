# About Zhi En Chuangke Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.

Zhi En Chuangke Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. is a technology service company based in Shenzhen and serving the Greater Bay Area (Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Macao). We focus on enterprise IT services and RPA-based process automation solutions.

[[toc]]

## Who we are

::: info Company profile
We position ourselves as a **long-term technical partner who understands your business**, not just an outsourcing team.
Whether it is an IT system or an RPA robot, everything comes down to one goal: making your business run **more stable, faster and with less manual burden**.
:::

- Aim for long-term partnership instead of one-off project delivery
