## RPA and automation scenarios

Based on **Laiye RPA** together with the Spring and Vue tech stacks, we design and implement stable, maintainable automation solutions for scenarios such as:

- Cross-border e-commerce: order synchronization, inventory updates, logistics tracking, ad and report consolidation
- Standalone sites: multi-channel data consolidation, user and order aggregation, campaign performance analysis
- Financial RPA: multi-ledger reconciliation, voucher generation, automated reporting and scheduling
- WeChat / Alipay mini programs: payment reconciliation, operations metrics aggregation and monitoring

::: info Typical value points
- Reduce labor involved in high-frequency, repetitive operations
- Reduce risks caused by manual data entry errors
- Let business teams focus on decisions and value creation instead of repetitive actions
:::

## IT and corporate web directions

For IT systems and web projects, our common project types include:

- Corporate websites and brand sites: multilingual support, SEO friendly, mobile-optimized
- Corporate communities and content platforms: articles or resource centers, membership systems and interaction modules
- Online service portals and middle platforms: customer entry, business intake, internal approvals and data queries

> These projects are typically delivered as an end-to-end package combining design, development, deployment and operations, with modules tailored to your needs.
