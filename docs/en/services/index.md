## IT Services

### Corporate websites & brand presence 🌐

- Information architecture and visual design based on your brand positioning
- Responsive experience across devices and regions
- SEO and performance optimization

::: info Suitable scenarios
- You need a professional, trustworthy corporate presence
- You need bilingual or multilingual sites to support overseas customers
- You need integration with back-office or third-party services (customer support, analytics, etc.)
:::

### Business systems & admin platforms 🧩

- Custom CRM and internal management systems
- Permission control, approval workflows and operation logs
- Dashboards and data visualization for decision making

::: tip What we focus on
- Data security and permission control
- Whether operations fit real-world workflows
- Whether reports can truly support management decisions
:::

### System operations & technical advisory 🛠️

- Server deployment and monitoring
- Performance bottleneck analysis and optimization
- Security protection and backup strategies

> [!NOTE]
> Many companies already have systems in place, but they become slower and more complex over time. In such cases, it is usually better to start from **diagnosis and optimization** rather than rewriting everything from scratch.

## RPA Automation

### Process discovery & solution design 🔍

- Identify repetitive, high-frequency and rule-based tasks
- Evaluate automation benefits and implementation cost
- Design a phased automation roadmap

### RPA robot development & deployment 🤖

- Build robots based on **Laiye RPA** as the platform
- Support cross-system operations (web, desktop apps, spreadsheets, etc.)
- Monitoring and alerting to ensure stable operation

### Training & continuous optimization 📈

- Training for business teams on how to use and maintain robots
- Continuous optimization as your processes and systems evolve
- Build a reusable, enterprise-level automation asset library

::: tip Typical scenarios
- **Cross-border e-commerce**: order synchronization, inventory updates, logistics tracking, ad and report consolidation
- **Standalone sites**: multi-channel data consolidation, user & order aggregation, campaign performance analysis
- **Financial RPA**: multi-ledger reconciliation, voucher generation, report preparation and scheduled delivery
- **WeChat / Alipay mini programs**: payment reconciliation, campaign metrics aggregation, operations monitoring data
:::
